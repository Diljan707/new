## 📺 INDIAN IPTV M3U PLAYLIST HUB

**Diljan707 IPTV Master Hub** is an Indian IPTV playlist project that organizes television channel entries in the M3U playlist format. The playlist is intended for compatible IPTV players and brings supported Indian TV channel categories together in one place.

Explore the **Indian IPTV M3U playlist**, Indian live TV channel listings, M3U playlist management, and streaming technology information.

### Playlist & Project Links

- **Live M3U Playlist:** https://diljan707.github.io/new/playlist.m3u
- **GitHub Repository:** https://github.com/diljan707/new
- **Project Website:** https://diljan707.github.io/new/

### Channel and Search Topics

The playlist project includes channel entries and categories associated with Indian television. Depending on the current playlist contents, relevant channel searches may include:

- Indian TV channels and Indian live TV
- JioTV-related channel listings
- Zee network channel listings
- VZY channel listings
- Hindi and Punjabi television channels
- Indian IPTV and M3U channel lists

Channel availability and individual stream access can change. Only use streams and sources you are authorized to access.
